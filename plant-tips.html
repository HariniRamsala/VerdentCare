Frequently Asked Care Topics

Click any category header below to expand detailed care instructions.

1. Stem Cuttings: Take a clean 4-6 inch node cutting right below a leaf joint using sterilized shears.

2. Water Propagation: Submerge the bottom node in clean filtered water. Change water every 3-5 days until roots reach 2 inches long.

3. Leaf Dusting: Wipe large leaves with a damp microfiber cloth monthly to allow maximum sunlight absorption.

Spring & Summer: Plants experience active growth and higher evaporation. Water more frequently and fertilize once monthly with a balanced 10-10-10 liquid fertilizer.

Fall & Winter: Plants enter dormancy with lower sunlight levels. Reduce watering frequency by half and suspend fertilization until spring.

Neem Oil Spray: Mix 1 tsp cold-pressed neem oil with 1/2 tsp mild liquid dish soap in 1 quart of warm water. Spray undersides of leaves weekly.

Fungus Gnats: Allow top 2 inches of soil to dry completely between waterings and use yellow sticky traps.
