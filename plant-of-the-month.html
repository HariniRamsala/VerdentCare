The Peace Lily

Elegant, resilient, and air-purifying. The Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum) is a botanical masterpiece that signals its hydration needs with gentle drooping leaves, making it a perfect companion for both novices and seasoned gardeners.

lightbulb Botanical Fun Fact

Peace Lilies aren't true lilies! They belong to the Araceae family and their white 'flower' is actually a specialized leaf called a spathe.

Step-by-Step Care Guide

How to Help Your Peace Lily Thrive

Follow these four golden rules to ensure year-round white blooms and dark glossy foliage.

1

Lighting Setup

Place in bright, filtered light 3 to 6 feet away from a window. Direct sunlight can scorch delicate leaves.

2

Watering Routine

Water thoroughly when the top 1 inch of soil feels dry to touch. The plant will tell you it is thirsty with a slight leaf drop.

3

Humidity Boost

Keep ambient humidity around 60%. Mist fronds regularly or place near a room humidifier in drier climates.

4

Pruning Blooms

Trim spent flower stalks at the soil line with clean shears to direct plant energy into new white spathes.
