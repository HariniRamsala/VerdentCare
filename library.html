Snake Plant Spotlight

Sansevieria Trifasciata

Renowned for its architectural appeal and near-indestructible nature, the Snake Plant is the ultimate beginner companion. Its upright, sword-like leaves add a modern touch to any space while actively purifying indoor air.

Growth Journey

eco

Spring/Summer Active Growth

New pups appear at the base. Water slightly more frequently.

spa

Fall/Winter Dormancy

Growth slows significantly. Reduce watering to prevent root rot.

warning Common Care Issues

  • sick Mushy, yellowing leaves: Overwatering. Allow soil to dry out completely between waterings.
  • dry Wrinkled leaves: Severe underwatering. Soak the soil thoroughly.
